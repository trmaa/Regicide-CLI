REGICIDE

It's time to destroy the Zar dinasty.

First the Js, then the Qs, and lastly the Ks.

Js have 10 HP, and 05 DP.
Qs have 20 HP, and 10 DP.
Ks have 30 HP, and 15 DP.

Each round you play a card from your hand.

First, the hability is activated:
	SPADES -> Reduce enemy damage.
	DIAMONDS -> Return discarted cads to the deck.
	CLUBS -> Doble damage this round.
	HEARTS -> Draw cards from the deck into your hand.

Then deal damage.

Third, absorbe damage by discarding cards.

Loop until all enemies are defeated.
